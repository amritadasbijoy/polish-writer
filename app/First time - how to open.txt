POLISH WRITER — HOW TO INSTALL

1. Drag "Polish Writer" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder, RIGHT-CLICK "Polish Writer" and choose "Open",
   then click "Open" again.
   (You only need to do this the first time. macOS shows a warning because this
   free app isn't registered with Apple — it is safe and open source:
   https://github.com/amritadasbijoy/polish-writer)

   If macOS still says it can't be opened: go to
   System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".

3. The Settings window opens. Follow the 3 steps:
   • Allow Accessibility (turn on "Polish Writer")
   • Choose your AI provider and model
   • Paste your API key → Test connection → Save

4. Select text in any app and press ⌥ Space (Option + Space),
   or click the ✨ button that appears after you select text.

Find Polish Writer any time from the ✍︎ icon in your menu bar.
